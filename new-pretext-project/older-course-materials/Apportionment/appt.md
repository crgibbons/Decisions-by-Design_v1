Apportionment is the mathematical term for the process of distibuting a fixed number of indivisible items among different entities.

Ex: Faculty Positions, Faculty Departments

Fairness Criteria:

- Proportionality: two quantities are proportional if they have the same relative size. <q>Apportionment should be proportional (in some way)</q>


Example.

 ## Congress

 House Seats: 435 for the 50 states (plus 6 nonvoting members representing territories, but we focus on 435 and the 50 states because that is related to what is mandated in the consitution)

 Standard Divisor: Total US population (in the 50 states) divided by Number of Seats
 Standard Quota: State Pop/Standard Divisor
